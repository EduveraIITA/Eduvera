# Individual assignment submissions and feedback

Accepted delivery contract, 23 October 2026. This extends learning beyond reported
practice completion. The owning implementation and its verification are recorded
separately in `IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md`.

## Person and outcome

An instructor publishes instructions to a defined learning audience. A learner
returns to a saved private draft, submits an exact text/file version and receives
a receipt. The named reviewer either completes their review or asks for a specific
revision. The learner submits another identifiable attempt when permitted; earlier
attempts and reviews remain intact. Each person sees their next action in Work and
can distinguish saved, submitted, waiting, changes requested and reviewed.

This is not the existing Diary completion flag, a private-note convention, a grade,
an acknowledgement, a verified physical event or a full group-assignment workflow.

## Simple experience with explicit policy

Reuse the current people, learning context, assignments and theme. The instructor
enters instructions and an optional due date; the publication review shows the real
audience and exact reviewer. Do not ask for Core behaviours, selectors, subscriptions
or an automation workflow. The explicit publication approves the supported route
under current authority. Missing prerequisites have an in-context continuation,
not an unrelated setup catalogue or a silently manufactured grant.

Supported submission choices belong to a versioned native publication policy,
not constants masquerading as institution rules. Keep uncommon options behind
Options, preserve a concise summary in publication review, and enforce the same
retained policy in the API and selected-world worker/runtime paths.

- A due date reports lateness. A separately configured cutoff ends submissions;
  a missing cutoff must not become an invented deadline.
- Text, files or either are distinct supported submission modes. Platform file
  safety/size limits are technical floors, not the institution's assessment rule.
- Attempt limits and how another attempt is granted are explicit. Initially
  supported modes must be named accurately; unavailable automatic/group/grading
  modes cannot be offered as working settings.
- Reviewer response has no deadline or expiry unless an actual approved policy
  supplies one. The learner's due date is not the reviewer's SLA.

The first context adapter is an existing class/term with optional subject. It is
a school specialization, not the universal definition of education. Keep audience
resolution and current context authority separate from immutable attempt/review
semantics. Course offerings, batches and independent learning groups require their
typed owner/authorization adapters; do not emulate them by relaxing class enrollment
constraints, fabricating a term or storing arbitrary graph metadata. These other
contexts remain an explicit delivery gate until implemented and tested.

## Native ownership and authority

Learning owns assignments, frozen published audience, learner-private drafts,
immutable file metadata, ordered submission manifests, attempts and append-only
reviews. Use same-school composite references and revision checks. Publication
freezes instructions and audience; later membership changes never rewrite an old
receipt or disclose an attempt to a newly enrolled learner.

`learning.assignments.manage` is a separate, class-scoped supported action, optionally
subject-qualified. No default or seed grants it. Current account/person identity,
membership, exact source authority and reviewer appointment are rechecked for
reads, writes and retained-command replay. A role title, administrator status,
attendance action or text-practice grant is not assignment review authority.
Learners write only their own permitted draft and submission; a linked guardian
may read permitted instructions, submitted attempts and feedback, not learner drafts
or submit on the learner's behalf. An unrelated teacher cannot read private work.

The exact reviewer is retained at publication. Revocation blocks their actions;
another officeholder or teacher never silently inherits the source. An explicit
review handover may change the current responsibility projection while retaining
the original reviewer and every subsequent change as immutable history. Group
submission/grading remain separate owning workflows.

### Explicit reviewer handover

A person with current protected campus setup authority may repair a published
assignment's reviewer through **Review handovers**. The view exposes assignment
identity, current reviewer, pending counts and eligible replacements, never private
drafts, submitted answers, attachment names or feedback. Setup authority does not
grant instruction publication or review authority. The replacement must already
have current review authority in the exact class/subject or offering and a current
canonical person/account endpoint; a learner in that frozen audience cannot become
the reviewer through this action.

One preview binds the assignment revision, exact latest attempts and current review
or requested-revision handoffs. The operator explicitly selects the next reviewer
and gives a reason. A changed preview requires fresh review; a matching record is
not command acceptance. One atomic owning command retains the responsibility change,
advances its source revision, supersedes obsolete open tasks through the Core port,
and reissues exact pending tasks. Existing learner revision tasks keep their
attributed request-for-changes feedback while receiving current source lineage.
Original instructions, audience, attempts, manifests, completed reviews and message
references are not rewritten. Future submissions use the newly named reviewer.

This follows the existing explicit reviewer Task policy, without an invented Offer,
extra acceptance ceremony, reminder or SLA. The task is assigned by the authorized
operator; the successor's actual review is a separate attributed response, not an
implied prior acceptance. Known acceptance followed by a failed read recovers only
by read; unknown outcome replays the exact command after current authority checks.
An exact receipt-absent monotonic preview/revision proof may release stale intent for
fresh review. The same source and immutable handover/message history operate in
an isolated world with no Live or sibling mutation.

## Commands and handoffs

Prepare typed `learning.assignment.*` commands through the existing gateway before
native effects. Use current selected-world identity/time/authority, stable command
and source IDs, expected revisions and an exact payload commitment. The narrow
receipt-read port discovers accepted retries only after current authorization;
changed payloads conflict and pending/unknown outcomes never authorize a new effect
under a different command. An uncertain mobile response retains the exact command
and original inputs for serialized replay; a generic refresh cannot unlock it.
Known acceptance followed by a failed refresh permits a read only. A typed native
proof, after exact receipt lookup, that a monotonic source revision makes the old
command permanently inapplicable may release the retained text for fresh review.
Accepted receipts take precedence over later source changes.

Publication explicitly configures the exact reviewer route. A learner's first
Submit explicitly records their return route for revision requests; passive audience
members do not need accounts at publication. Configuration and selectors are source-
specific so one assignment cannot retire another's route. Explicit null timing
means no response SLA or expiry; omission is not an approved default.

Submission atomically retains the immutable attempt, exact manifest, event/outcome
and review handoff. A reviewer acts against that exact attempt; a stale review
cannot complete a newer submission. Request changes requires useful feedback and
creates learner work. Reviewed closes review work; feedback alone is an Update,
not an invented acknowledgement task. Generic Work carries source IDs, neutral
status and exact links, never submission text, file names or private feedback.
Current native authority and exact open protocol eligibility are both required for
an actionable handoff. Expired or closed protocol work cannot reappear as actionable
merely because its native attempt still awaits review; the learner sees the actual
unavailable route rather than a false completion. Event delivery/replay rechecks
current source, person, class/subject permission or exact learner/guardian access;
a historical routing audience alone is not current access.

## Artifacts and Simulation

Use the existing environment-bound artifact port. Stable file IDs and content
hashes bind bytes to the draft/attempt. Upload rejection or interrupted storage
must not publish nonexistent accepted evidence. Detaching a draft file cannot
remove immutable attempt evidence. Downloads recheck the exact audience and use
Unicode-safe attachment disposition, bounded safe types and untrusted-content
handling. Downloaded content must match the retained byte length and content hash;
corrupted or replaced bytes are unavailable, not accepted evidence. This check
must not turn a visibly omitted Simulation placeholder into a Live file read.

Simulation captures authorized metadata without copying Live document bytes or
paths. Omitted source bytes remain visibly omitted. A new branch submission cannot
pass an omitted placeholder off as a fresh uploaded artifact; replace it with a
run-local upload. Run-local files, commands, routes, Work and outcomes stay isolated,
with no Live read fallback. Captured historic receipts remain identifiable history.

## Acceptance

Prove ordinary instructor/learner handoffs, durable draft/reload, text and file
manifests, submission receipt, revision request/resubmission and review closure.
Include stale revisions, duplicate/changed-payload retries, current permission and
endpoint revocation, guardian/other-learner privacy, omitted/corrupt/interrupted
artifacts and meaningful COW changes leaving Live and sibling state untouched.
Use the common mobile UI in all four appearances, with pending/navigation and
accepted-versus-unconfirmed recovery. Group work, grading, offering/batch adapters,
hosted artifact/provider checks and physical-device approval remain named gates.

## Learning group first use

Accepted bounded extension, 23 October 2026; checkpoint 76's local delivery and
remaining acceptance gates are recorded in the implementation plan. This introduces a second
typed assignment context, `learning_offering`, rather than renaming a school class.

An operator chooses **Assignments**, names one learning group, selects or adds
canonical People and explicitly chooses its instructor. The review states who
will be able to publish assignments and review work. Saving the group creates its
native learning registrations and dated teaching appointment, then opens the
ordinary assignment workspace. No class, term, academic year, admission number,
employment, guardian, timetable or Attendance setup is required. A passive learner
can be included without an account; app invitation remains a separate optional
Live action against that same person.

The first form asks for the group name, people and instructor only. It reuses the
shared Add people/import/collision sheet, keeps search conditional for small
rosters and shows a concise review before creating responsibility. Dates and
uncommon details are disclosed when needed. The selected-world date, not the
device clock, supplies today's effective start. Existing groups/assignments open
their exact sources; back, reload, cancellation and uncertain writes must not
create a replacement or discard accepted work.

Learning owns the offering, explicit person registrations and purpose-specific
teaching appointments. These are learning facts, not another person directory or
an arbitrary descriptive-group permission system. Existing People groups may
supply a reviewed initial roster in a future adapter; later descriptive membership
changes cannot silently enroll someone or change published audience. The bounded
initial direct roster has at most 100 explicitly reviewed people.

Current protected campus-setup authority may record an explicit teaching
appointment through the owning command, as it may assign supported technical work
responsibilities. Administrator status alone is never publish/review authority.
The actual instructor is a canonical person with the named dated assignment;
acting also requires their current account/person endpoint. This does not grant
an institutional office, financial decision power or a standing mandate, and does
not claim the full Model Core first-institution authority ceremony is implemented.

This first offering adapter supports one current instructor with app access. The
operator reviews that real person and the exact roster before creating the group;
they are not automatically assigned to themselves. Instructor and learner must be
different people in this adapter: self-assessment and dual-role review need an
explicit assessment policy, not an implied permission. This is a named delivery
limit, not a rule imposed on every campus. A manager may end an appointment and
explicitly appoint a replacement for new assignments. Published authors remain
unchanged. Previously published reviews move only through the explicit source-bound
review handover above; a replacement appointment alone never grants that historical
private work. Until handover, outstanding work remains visibly waiting.

An active neutral app member may submit their own offering work when the current
person endpoint, frozen audience and exact effective registration allow it. No
student profile is manufactured. Guardian access remains the existing class
adapter's separate contract; offering membership grants no new family disclosure.
An ended registration blocks new writes but retains that person's permitted
submitted history. Rejoining creates a new registration and must not reopen old
published work automatically. Revoked instructors lose private read/review access;
an old appointment is not a permanent historical staff entitlement.

The same immutable assignment/attempt/review lifecycle supports both contexts.
Retain all existing IDs, student/class bookmarks, file paths, manifests and accepted
command commitments. Add canonical-person and registration evidence through a
reviewed additive migration; never invent identity or rewrite submitted content
while backfilling. New person-qualified work/file routes are distinct from the old
student-qualified routes. The owning source selects the adapter, not a guessed ID.

The Assignments starting goal resolves current actor/world, permitted action,
exact source/revision and missing prerequisites. An owned saved source stays first
use; accepted publication, not a context count, advances the owning continuation.
An arbitrary URL cannot grant access, another operator cannot overwrite the owner's
landing and Simulation cannot write Live's personal onboarding preferences.

Setup, registration, appointment and assignment commands use the existing typed
Core command/event/outcome ports, exact receipt recovery and current authorization.
Version source-aware events and realtime predicates without breaking legacy replay.
The same APIs and screens operate on an isolated COW world with run-local context,
people, authority, files and work; real account invitation stays Live-only. Acceptance
requires ordinary instructor/neutral-member handoffs, privacy/revocation/rejoin,
stale/unknown/replayed commands, legacy history, actual COW mutation and unchanged
Live/sibling records, plus all four mobile appearances. Group submission, grades,
program/qualification hierarchy and competency judgement are not this extension.

## Research informing supported variation

[Moodle assignment settings](https://docs.moodle.org/503/en/Assignment_settings)
separate due/cutoff dates, text/file modes, draft submission and attempt reopening.
These are examples of variation, not universal defaults for ThatCampus.
[IIT Madras academics](https://study.iitm.ac.in/ds/academics.html) describes separate
course registrations and mixed online/in-person assessments. This supports retaining
typed learning contexts instead of treating one school class as every learner's
academic world. These implementation choices are our inference from those examples.

The [Open University distance-learning FAQ](https://www.open.ac.uk/courses/what-is-distance-learning/faqs/)
describes module-by-module registration, different module starts and a tutor for
each module. Our prospective `module_presentation` adapter therefore needs its
own registration and tutor responsibility, separate from qualification membership.
The FAQ also distinguishes assignment resubmission from exam or end-of-module
reassessment; those must not become one generic retry switch.

The [NSW Training Services training-plan guideline](https://www.nsw.gov.au/education-and-training/resources/vtgs/vtg-training-plan)
describes learner/employer/training-provider plans, competency units, agreed
variations and separate supervisor endorsement and provider assessment. Our
prospective training-plan adapter must keep submitted evidence, workplace
endorsement and assessor judgement distinct, with current purpose-specific
authority. Neither adapter is implemented. These are examples informing the
extension boundary, not jurisdictional rules or new default campus configuration.
