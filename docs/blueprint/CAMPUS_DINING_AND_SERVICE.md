## First implementation boundary

The owner authorised implementation and reasserted the true agent-based,
system-of-systems model on 24 October. The implemented source foundation is native
dated menus/service hours, explicit service-state and cumulative-count observations,
reasoned corrections and exact-person shortage handling. Menu ownership and serving
ownership independently support native operation or labelled external references;
no transactional provider connector, financial flow or named meal entitlement is
claimed in this slice.

The bounded native path includes:

- One service with a named coordinator and optional canonical usual place; dated
  sessions pin their service revision, coordinator, operator, place and ownership
  modes rather than rewriting history when defaults change.
- Draft, explicit publication, reasoned menu/hour changes and human-recorded
  open/pause/resume/end/cancel transitions. A draft or service-reference save is not
  the first operation; accepted native menu publication is.
- Cumulative anonymous reported serving totals, observation times and exact
  reasoned count corrections. An absent observation displays as “Not recorded”,
  not zero.
- Source-owned shortage/problem reports with exact handler acceptance, resolution,
  reopening, reassignment and unavailable-route recovery. Ending a service does not
  close unresolved issues, create a Repair or order replacement supplies.
- Source-backed Needs me/Waiting Tasks and exact session/issue links in the shared
  Work/Areas shell. A current assigned coordinator, operator or handler can act only
  within that source; membership or a job title does not confer Dining management.
- Dining-only progressive start and the same owning services, selected-world clock,
  authority and run-local stores in Simulation. Final current-source execution and
  UI acceptance are tracked in the acceptance record, not inferred from registration.

Menu and serving modes independently select `native` or `reference`. The initial
reference path records one external-system metadata bundle per service and an
explicitly labelled link, not a synced menu, provider status or transactional API.
Fully reference-only services do not manufacture local dated operations. Modes
cannot silently change after dated records exist; general cutover is not delivered.
External actions are unavailable in Simulation.

Named diners/entitlements, reservations/orders, dietary approval, demand/preparation
planning, billing, stock and supplier accounting are not included. Recurring menus
are the separate approved increment below. Neither the native stores nor their
receipts replace those owners.

The canonical institution/campus → building → space/room structure remains owned
by topology. Resources retain their existing identity and dated placement; humans
retain canonical People identities and explicit acting-agent endpoints. Dining
references that structure and contributes source-qualified context through the
existing journal/coordination ports, not copied rooms, resources or people.
Spaces/resources are entities; people, registered
devices/integrations and configured software are agents. A planned assignment,
reported presence and observed food handoff are distinct relationships/evidence.
Anonymous totals never create human occupants or automatic stock movements. Full
Dining temporal relationships remain future work; current spatial context is not a
claim that every planned or observed relationship has been projected.

Service hours are non-reserving availability, not room bookings or staff shifts.
The weekly increment adds the shared Calendar projection described below;
conflict-aware reservations remain a gap. There is no second recurrence engine or
parallel writable Timetable. Existing residential-use protections remain intact.

## Weekly menu and availability increment

Owner approved 25 October 2026; bounded local acceptance passed at release 123. A person
responsible for a canteen or mess can plan one named recurring service period,
select its weekdays, reuse its place/operator and set menu/time variations by day.
The default remains a single date. Weekly publication has an explicit first date
and optional last date; an open-ended plan is not forced to stop after a preview
horizon. The review shows concrete upcoming dates before publication.

The normal path is Service → Plan menu → Once or Weekly → Review and publish.
Opening one date shows that date's current menu, hours and source. **Change this
date** changes only that occurrence through the native dated-session owner;
the weekly plan and other dates remain unchanged. Cancellation retains a dated
exception rather than allowing the base pattern to reappear. Already recorded
serving, shortages and correction history cannot be overwritten by a series edit.

The authoritative weekly plan retains typed weekday/time/menu records, immutable
publication evidence, canonical person/place references and revision lineage.
Shared scheduling utilities own bounded once/weekly expansion and timezone
resolution. No read creates a dated session, work item, observation or agent
decision. Explicit dated commands retain deterministic occurrence identity and
fresh authority/source checks. Drafts, duplicate/stale commands, invalid clock
changes and incomplete responsibility routes have visible recovery paths.

Published food availability contributes a typed, source-backed read projection to
Calendar and the exact responsible person's Today context, with an owning Dining
link. It is not a room reservation, attendance expectation, accepted staff shift,
named meal entitlement or evidence that service opened. Public projections omit
private counts, issues and correction notes. A cancelled unpublished draft remains
private. Dining-only entry must not require or grant Timetable management.

Native/reference capability ownership remains independent. An external menu cannot
be republished locally as if it were native, and a reference-only operator cannot
acquire local serving actions through a weekly plan. The same accepted plan,
bounded expansion, one-date exceptions and source-qualified reads/commands operate
inside isolated Simulation, with unchanged Live and sibling state.
The [weekly acceptance record](../VERIFICATION.md#dining) owns the
current release gate, rather than the earlier dated-session checkpoint.

## People and outcomes first

This table describes the broader target. The first boundary above does not yet
deliver reservations, named redemption, forecasting or financial continuation.

| Person / situation | What they should understand and do | Meaningful completion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Diner or authorised guardian | Find today's menu, place, serving hours, latest changes and applicable cost; reserve or ask only where needed. | A published answer, confirmed reservation/collection, or a clear pending request—not a generic success message. |
| Planner, committee member or chef | Draft a day or menu cycle, check feasibility, obtain any required approval and publish; change an unavailable item with reason. | The current dated version is visible, with affected bookings/people handled according to explicit rules. |
| Operator or supervisor | See expected demand, preparation reports, accepted staff duties, current service and unresolved problems. Open, pause, resume and end service explicitly. | Service ended with recorded evidence; unfinished repairs, reconciliation or bills remain separately owned. |
| Counter worker | Record the actual handoff using the configured count or named-entitlement method, including assisted service without a phone. | The observation/redemption is saved, retry-safe and correctable; it does not claim that food was eaten. |
| Institutional coordinator | Review exceptions, provider changes, complaints and handovers without managing every transaction. | Named owners and current accepted arrangements are clear. A delivered message is not accepted work. |
| Accounts / supplier handler | Check a reviewed charge basis or supplier bill, handle a query and reconcile through the financial owner. | A source-linked financial outcome, not a Dining “paid” checkbox. |

A dietary request awaiting human review, a packed meal ordered but uncollected,
an absent child with an uncancelled booking, an unavailable kitchen and an incorrect
second charge are normal exception journeys, not hidden administrative settings.

## Compose services instead of imposing one mess model

A Dining service describes an accountable operation; it is not identical to a
hostel, room, contractor, meal plan or payment account. One service may use several
canonical locations or serve several residences. One campus may combine student-run,
in-house and contracted outlets. Changes of operator/location are effective-dated;
they must not erase prior service or billing evidence.

Configure only the dimensions a campus uses. These are target design choices;
the implemented foundation exposes only the bounded native/reference modes and
dated menu/service/count/issue responsibilities described above:

| Dimension | Target design choices, not implemented configurations or active defaults |
| --- | --- |
| Operational ownership | Institution, student committee, contractor or explicitly divided responsibilities. Per-capability native/connected/reference owner. |
| Preparation / fulfilment | On-site/off-site; buffet, counter, pre-order, packed-meal collection, delivery or event catering. |
| Audience / access | Open service, explicit people or dated groups, approved plan, guest or sponsored access. Descriptive membership alone grants nothing. |
| Reservation | None, optional or required; meal reservation versus dish choice; capacity, cutoff and cancellation arrangements. |
| Financial basis | Included, free/sponsored, fixed subscription, per-meal or per-item. Stored-value credit needs a separate reviewed financial owner. |
| Service calendar | User-named meal periods, dates/cycles, serving and opening hours, closures, overnight windows and one-off exceptions. No fixed three-meal/day assumption. |
| Evidence grain | Anonymous service counts or named entitlement/redemption, selected explicitly. Not every meal needs a named diner. |
| Oversight | Direct operation or reviewed publication/guest/substitution/billing handoffs, with scope and correction rules. |

Optional starter configurations are visible, editable drafts—not activated hidden
automations. Missing routing/behaviour configuration records the missing setup or
uses an explicitly configured human fallback; Core does not guess “the Warden”.

## Native ownership and current gaps

Reuse canonical People, Groups, spaces and resource holdings. Do not create a
second resident directory, kitchen inventory or vendor employee identity system.
Passive diners need no app accounts. An authorised on-behalf action is explicit;
guardian relationship, job title and topology setup access are not sufficient authority.

The current native owner retains services, dated sessions and issues with immutable
receipts. The wider Dining-owned concepts below are semantic boundaries, not a
claim that all exist or a prescribed generic attribute/workflow schema:

- Service and effective operational responsibilities, plus source-qualified capability
  ownership where connected.
- Menu version, dated service instance and publication/exception evidence.
- Forecast or expectation snapshot, labelled by method and time.
- Optional reservation, explicit entitlement/guest authorisation and private request.
- Append-only serving/redemption observations with quantity and correction lineage.
- Reviewed billing basis and exact links to the owning Finance/provider outcomes.

The existing topology can represent named dining/kitchen locations as `other` today;
the native Dining owner now references those canonical locations. A fixture's Dining
Hall alone is still not evidence of an operated service. New purpose/space semantics
need review.
Resources owns equipment identity; Loans owns custody; Repairs owns fault handling.
Ingredient lots, units, expiry, purchasing, supplier payables and stored-value wallets
are not delivered by those owners and must not be fabricated from pooled holdings.

## DT handles the process, not just a retrospective mirror

Every material action follows the existing model-mediated chain:

Identified command or registered observation → current authority, source owner,
rule/configuration and revision checks → prepared typed domain command → owning
transaction and immutable evidence/outbox → correlated outcome → configured exact
next-person/provider handoff → response and fresh checks.

The first slice routes shortage handling as a typed Task to the exact source-owned
handler. The handler's acceptance and native resolution are separate from message
delivery, and publication changes remain independently authorised actions.

A broader future example is a cook reporting a shortage against today's service.
An effective rule could route a substitution decision to the named planner, whose
authorised decision publishes a new dated menu version. Affected reservations or
operator work would receive their own required continuation. A diner
receiving that update does not automatically accept a new paid order or diet change.
If menus belong to a vendor, the request goes to that source and stays pending
until an accepted provider response or authorised reconciliation is recorded.

Retain tenant/world, concrete actor/source, expected revision, command/idempotency
key, effective/observed/recorded times, schema and rule versions, correlation,
causation, private evidence references and supersession. All mutable routing,
deadlines, retries and escalation follow approved effective configuration. The
kernel remains behaviour-neutral; Dining semantics remain in typed domain adapters.

## Keep independent facts independent

Menu published ≠ expected diners ≠ prepared portions ≠ entered ≠ collected/served
≠ billable usage ≠ charge issued ≠ payment reported ≠ verified receipt.
This is a dependency warning, not a chain that automatically advances.

- Publication is versioned; later substitutions do not rewrite what was previously
  offered or recorded as handed over. Diet/allergen information needs current human
  provenance, not inherited assurances from a replaced ingredient.
- The first slice distinguishes draft/publication from open/paused/ended/cancelled
  service transitions with explicit evidence.
  Clock expiry alone proves neither opening nor closure. Preparation readiness is
  not a food-safety certification. Financial issues may outlive physical service.
- A future forecast records assumptions and input revisions. A reservation does
  not prove collection; residence/attendance does not prove entitlement or absence
  rebate.
- The first slice records cumulative count-only totals, not incremental additions.
  Cumulative updates supersede their previous value, not add it again. They cannot identify diners,
  prevent individual repeat meals or justify named charges.
- Future named mode must validate the exact entitlement/service and configured
  permitted units.
  Lock/uniqueness semantics must prevent concurrent counters spending the same
  allowance, even with different command IDs. Unlimited service is not arbitrarily
  reduced to one helping by a universal duplicate rule.
  With an external allowance owner, require its authoritative atomic redemption
  operation or an explicitly delegated, non-overlapping quota with reconciliation.
  A read-only balance connection cannot confirm a local redemption while another
  counter/provider can spend the same units. Native locking alone is insufficient.
- A mistaken serving observation is corrected with actor, reason and linkage.
  Restoring an allowance or changing a charge is a separate authorised decision;
  a void must not silently assert that handed-over food was returned.
- Guest access, sponsor identity, payer responsibility and an approved subsidy are
  separate choices. No name match or informal invitation creates liability.

## Shared calendar, not a second timetable

Published food availability is not automatically an exclusive hall reservation.
Service hours, outlet opening hours and a meal plan's redemption period are distinct.
The implemented dated sessions reuse shared timezone resolution, reject invalid
local times and require explicit ambiguous-time choices. The weekly adapter uses
the shared recurrence primitive and projects typed `availability` context. It
creates no room reservation, staff duty or required participant. Overlapping
service hours therefore do not mean a booking conflict has been checked or cleared.
Date exceptions, immutable revisions and explicit clock-time resolution stay in
Dining; Calendar is a read projection, not another writer or a teaching activity.

Existing exclusive claim sources keep their current conflict rules. Availability
cannot acquire people through optional-participant flags and is excluded from
occupancy, required-work and teaching KPI interpretation. Exclusive Dining
reservations would require a separate reviewed command/booking contract, including
explicit closures, maintenance and required staff duties. Current publication is
not an exclusive window, seating allocation or venue clearance.

Diners' optional attendance is not a staff duty assignment. Staff shifts, exclusive
event use, equipment custody, portions available and seating capacity have different
owners/units. Kitchen downtime can prompt an explicit service decision and linked
Repair; a fault closure cannot automatically prove service resumed. Bedroom delivery
does not bypass dedicated residential-use protections or create a room booking.

## Finance and external providers

Follow [External-system coexistence](EXTERNAL_SYSTEM_COEXISTENCE.md). A campus may
retain its canteen POS/app and payment ERP, operate Dining natively, or replace one
capability later. Native menus do not require native payments, and a connected
payment processor does not make ThatCampus the invoice or settlement owner.

The first slice creates no Dining charge basis, Finance handoff, provider order,
payment or refund. The following describes the required boundary for a future
financial continuation, not a delivered Dining billing path.

For native Finance, use its charge, payment review, receipt and correction owners.
Current batches support a uniform amount per reviewed person, not arbitrary
per-person item totals in one atomic call. Existing public charge methods own their
transactions. Dining therefore needs exact source-qualified charge-basis links and
durable continuation/recovery, or a reviewed Finance composition adapter; it cannot
claim atomic “served + charged + paid”. Quantity/pricing and currency snapshots,
already-billed protection and explicit credit/refund treatment require acceptance
tests. No automatic bill arises merely from a headcount or attendance mark.

A UPI screenshot, till entry or successful provider redirect is not independently
verified settlement. Supplier bills and diner invoices are different obligations.
Subscriptions, absence rebates, break closures, instrument expiry and rollover are
dated terms, not universal defaults. Free/included or information-only service can
start without configuring Finance at all.

## Small setup, compact screens

Start with a service name, an accountable person and the place/hours/menu needed
for the chosen outcome. No mandatory hostel tree, academic term, stock catalogue,
vendor contract, wallet or full plan configuration. Explain capability ownership
only when a person chooses to connect an existing system; do not make integration
setup part of a diner's or counter worker's day.

Owner clarification, 25 October 2026: meal-by-meal joining and cancellation are
not the default next increment. Start from the actual operator decision and the
least input needed to support it. A useful preparation expectation may be one
operator-entered count, a stable arrangement or an authorised existing source;
it need not become a named participation ledger. Roster- or history-based estimates
must show their basis and are not confirmed diners, entitlement, service or billing
evidence. Reuse standing arrangements and record material changes or exceptions
rather than require repeated student taps. Named reservations or redemption need a
demonstrated operational reason, such as a limited paid booking or packed-meal
collection, and their own reviewed authority/evidence contract. Do not activate
extra tracking or automation just to complete the model.
Service controls and count recording are capabilities, not a mandatory daily
checklist or a prerequisite to useful menus and hours. Where the campus does not
record an actual service fact, retain unknown rather than manufacture completion.

Use the current shell and restrained compact theme, not a separate canteen app.
The implemented Area has date/service discovery, dated menu/hours and state,
source-owned running/count actions, shortage work, compact history and explicit
command-outcome recovery. Its reference views say that the provider is not
connected or synced. The wider screen responsibilities remain targets:

- Dining today: current outlet/date, menu and serving hours, concise changed/closed
  information and applicable cost. Viewing may require no action; show a relevant
  reserve/collect/help action only where the selected operation needs it.
- Run service: expected versus recorded counts with labels, a compact current-state
  summary, the next authorised action and unresolved work. A connected owner shows
  provenance/freshness and a source action where supported.
- Menu and hours: one day/cycle editor, preview and publication path, plus dated
  exceptions. More policy is disclosed only when needed.
- Review: exact affected service/person/item, concise difference, explicit decision
  and correction. Private dietary evidence is not a generic expandable public row.

Informative explanations use the shared information affordance. Actions remain
reachable above mobile keyboards; no swipe-only controls, decorative status panels
or mandatory repeated configuration. Verify Light/Dark, blue Live and amber
Simulation before a UI delivery claim; current review evidence and remaining
physical-device/owner acceptance are recorded separately in the acceptance report.

## Privacy and degraded operation

Public menu labels and a private dietary accommodation are different records.
Retain only necessary sensitive information, route to an authorised human team and
show kitchen staff only the agreed operational arrangement. No medical evidence in
public rosters, generic Core payloads or broad Work notifications. Do not infer
allergen safety or provide automated medical approval.

Anonymous manual counts do not require diner accounts or phones. Offline capture,
sync and named phone-free redemption are not delivered in the first slice.
For any future offline path, reports must remain provisional with source,
observation time and reconciliation ownership. Disconnected counters
cannot generally guarantee globally once-only entitlement redemption; disclose the
limitation and reconcile conflicts rather than promise prevention. A stale balance
is labelled stale. No hidden outage fallback creates verified payment, automatic
charges or a new write owner. Emergency response follows the campus's applicable
human procedure; the app is not a food-safety authority.
